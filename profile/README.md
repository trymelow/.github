A music player for people who listen a lot and sing along. No banners, no clips between tracks, no skip limits and no lyrics behind a paywall — the music, and the words exactly in time.

### Inside

| Feature | What you get |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Lyrics in time** | Each word lights up the moment it's sung, letter by letter, over the cover's colours. |
| **The catalogue** | Search an artist, an album or a track, or paste a link — it plays in a second. |
| **Moving over** | Paste a playlist link from Spotify, Yandex Music, VK Music, SoundCloud or YouTube Music, and the same playlist appears in your library. |
| **Share links** | A track, an album or a playlist opens from a link for anyone, with or without Melow. |
| **Everywhere you sign in** | Likes, playlists and follows are the same on every device you sign in on. |
| **Nothing extra** | No tracking unless you turn it on; audio and covers come through Melow's own servers. Eleven languages. |
